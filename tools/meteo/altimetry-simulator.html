Altimetry simulator · helicopter

TA = IA + (QNH − QNH2) × 27 + [ 0.004 × ISA DEV × ΔALT ]
where ΔALT = IA − station elevation (height above the QNH source)
Blue = pressure correction (QNH) · Orange = temperature correction · Green = QNH station. TA true altitude · IA indicated altitude · QNH actual sea-level pressure from the station · QNH2 altimeter setting · ISA DEV deviation from ISA (°C) · ΔALT height used for the temperature error (ft). The ISA deviation is assumed constant between the station and the aircraft.

Exercise (Mont Blanc). A helicopter crosses a mountain range with the altimeter set to 1013 hPa (flight level) and reads 18000 ft. The QNH, measured at a sea-level station, is 983 hPa and the temperature is ISA −25 °C. What is its true altitude? Does it clear the 15872 ft peak?

Solid helicopter = real position (TA). Dashed = what the pilot reads (IA). From indicated to true there are two steps: pressure first (blue), then temperature (orange). The gray bracket is ΔALT, measured from the QNH station.
